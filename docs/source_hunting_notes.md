# Source-hunting notes (Phase 1)

Findings from a first pass at resolving the bare-root portals in
`config/domains.yaml`. All via web search, not yet fetched/verified by
running `fetch_sources.py` — treat as leads to confirm, not settled facts.

## Land & Property — resolved

`dolma.gov.np/faqs` is a real, live official FAQ page with substantive
procedural content on land ownership transfer (नामसारी). Added to config.
A mirror at `public.dolma.gov.np/faqs` and several per-district subdomains
(e.g. `dharan.dolma.gov.np/faqs`, a district land office FAQ) also exist —
worth a quick check on whether district pages add anything the national
page doesn't, since your proposal's document metadata schema already has
room for this (issuing authority, version).

## Employment & Labour — partially resolved, needs a human pass

- `dofe.gov.np` confirmed as the real official DoFE site (don't trust
  SEO-aggregator sites that mislabel it as a forestry department — that's
  an aggregator error, not a fact about the real site).
- No clean on-site FAQ page surfaced in search, unlike dolma.gov.np. Added
  the Downloads page as a placeholder source, but someone on the team
  should browse `dofe.gov.np` directly (आफ्नो बारे / स्रोत sections) —
  search engines under-index Nepali government sites, so "not found by
  search" isn't strong evidence "doesn't exist."
- `ssf.gov.np` similarly has no obvious FAQ page — its real procedural
  content seems to live in About/Board pages and PDF circulars rather than
  FAQs. `sosys.ssf.gov.np` is the transactional login portal, not
  informational content, so it's not a good ingestion source for Q&A.
- Third-party site `bizsewa.com` has a fairly thorough SSF FAQ compilation.
  **Do not ingest it directly** — your proposal's Legal section commits to
  official sources only. It's useful only as a checklist of *question
  types* to go looking for on ssf.gov.np itself.

## A discovery worth investigating: existing Nepali gov-service Q&A datasets

Search turned up two Hugging Face datasets — `teksingh/NagarGPT-dataset1`
and `teksingh/Refactor-dataset` — containing Nepali question-answer pairs
that look like they were built from DoNIDCR content (birth registration,
marriage registration document requirements) and possibly other municipal
FAQ sources.

**This could genuinely help your project**, but treat it carefully before
using it for anything beyond inspiration:

1. **Provenance is unverified from here.** It's not clear whether these
   Q&A pairs were scraped verbatim from official pages, paraphrased by a
   human, or generated by an LLM. Your proposal requires answers grounded
   in *verified official* documents — if this dataset's answers were
   themselves LLM-generated, using it as ground truth would import
   exactly the hallucination risk your project is designed to reduce.
2. **Possible use, if provenance checks out:** as a source of realistic
   *everyday-phrasing* questions (this is literally your Introduction's
   core problem — bridging informal Nepali phrasing to formal procedure
   text) for the domain-classifier training set or the 100-question eval
   set — NOT as a substitute for your own curated knowledge base.
3. Before using it at all: open the dataset on Hugging Face, check its
   dataset card for a stated source/methodology, and spot-check a handful
   of answers against the actual DoNIDCR pages they claim to reflect.
